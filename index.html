Creative statement

I’m interested in how small design choices affect the way an image feels. Games and digital worlds shape how I look at visual media. I notice the colors, lighting, interfaces, and details that make a place recognizable.

Through photography and digital design, I’m learning to make those choices deliberately. Contrast can pull a subject forward, space can change the balance of a composition, and color can change the mood of a scene. I want to understand how these elements work together instead of making choices only because they look good.

This website brings together my work for MEDPL 150. My goal is to improve how I use images and design principles to communicate a clear idea, while developing a visual style that reflects my own interests.
